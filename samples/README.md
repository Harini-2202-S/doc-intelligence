# Sample documents

Generated by `python scripts/make_samples.py`. Kestrel Freight Pvt Ltd and every person, vendor, address and bank
detail in these files are fictional. They are laid out like real company documents: cover page, document control
and revision history, contents page, numbered sections, tables, running headers and footers with page numbers,
sign-off blocks and annexures.

Upload all ten into one collection, for example "Kestrel Freight". Skip this README.

## Things to try

**Briefs.** Open the meeting minutes: the actions table should list Arjun, Priya, Kavya, TrackPoint and Neha
with due dates. Open the vendor register: facts should include contract values and notice deadlines. Open the
board deck: the revenue chart's monthly figures are searchable because chart data is extracted.

**Ask (whole collection)**
- "How many days of annual leave do employees get?" A good answer cites both 21 and 18 days and points out the conflict.
- "Within how many days must I submit an expense claim?" (30 vs 45)
- "How long must a password be?" (12 vs 14)
- "Who approves an expense claim of Rs. 7,00,000?" The app should say it couldn't find this.
- "Which vendor contracts have notice deadlines before January 2027?"
- "What was revenue in September and how does it compare with last year?" (from the chart)
- "When does Project Atlas launch, and what could delay it?" (deck plus minutes)
- "Which actions are overdue?" (minutes action tracker)

**Ask (one document).** Open `leave_policy_v4.pdf`, click "Ask about this document", ask "What is the
carry-forward limit?" (5 days).

**Security.** Open the supplier quote: a banner shows the hidden instruction, and asking "Should we approve the
Bluewave quote?" should not repeat it.

**Citations.** Click any citation: the page opens with the exact passage highlighted, and the page number matches
the real PDF page.

Edge-case files for testing rejections and attacks are in `test_cases/` (generate them with
`python scripts/make_test_cases.py --oversized`). See TESTING.md.
